What we do

We work in mild, alloy and stainless steel, aluminium, brass, bronze and many plastics, and keep a wide stock of materials for emergencies.

Breakdown repairs

Broken shafts, hubs that need boring and sleeving, and worn parts built up and machined back, turned round quickly.

Turning

Conventional lathes up to 2540 mm between centres with a 1080 mm swing, and CNC lathes for batch work.

Milling

Manual millers up to 1500 x 750 mm and CNC millers, from keyways and slots to fully machined parts.

CNC and grinding

CNC turning and milling for batches in the hundreds or thousands, and surface and cylindrical grinding to 0.005 mm.

Welding and fabrication

TIG and arc welding in steel, stainless and aluminium, plus metal spraying, brazing, plasma cutting and heat treatment.

Bending and cutting

A 300 ton CNC press brake and a large guillotine, both taking steel up to 12 mm thick and 3 metres long.

About CMS Wrexham

CMS Wrexham has been on Wrexham Industrial Estate for 30 years, giving companies a specialist engineering service. We handle emergency breakdowns and larger projects, from the first design to the finished part.

Our technicians are trained on a wide range of machines, and many of the team have over 40 years in engineering. One of our younger members came second in the Apprentice of the Year awards.
