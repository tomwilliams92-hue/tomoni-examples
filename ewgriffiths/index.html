What we do

Homes, businesses and community buildings, from the groundwork to the finishing touches.

New builds

New homes, including timber frame houses. At Gwern, Nefyn, we demolished the old building and finished the new house in 16 weeks.

Barn and farm conversions

Old farm buildings turned into homes and offices, keeping their stone character.

Extensions and conservatories

Extensions, garages and conservatories, clad in local stone to match the old building.

Renovations

Old houses and cottages brought up to date, with oak kitchens, stairs, windows and doors made by us.

Groundwork and landscaping

Groundwork, footings and landscaping, so the job is finished outside as well as in.

Machine hire

Diggers from 0.9 to 13.5 tons, a telehandler, a roller and a dumper, on flexible terms.

About E.W. Griffiths

E.W. Griffiths has been building on the Llŷn Peninsula since 1991, and became a limited company in 2003. Our work ranges from renovations and extensions to converting old farm buildings into homes, and more recently new builds and commercial and industrial work.

As a small firm we give each project close attention from start to finish, and many clients ask us back. At Crowrach Uchaf, Bwlchtocyn, we've returned for several jobs, from converting the outbuildings to a new conservatory and kitchen.
