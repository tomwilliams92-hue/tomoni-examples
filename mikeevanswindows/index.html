What we do

Everything is surveyed, measured and made to order, and meets building regulations before it's fitted.

Windows

Casement, tilt and turn and bow windows in uPVC, in white, light oak or woodgrain.

Doors

Front and back doors, French doors and two, three or four-panel patio doors, with a choice of colours and glass.

Conservatories

Victorian, Edwardian and sun-lounge styles. We do the whole build, including the base, electrics, plumbing and tiling.

Porches

Brick or glazed uPVC porches, designed and made to measure for your home, and built to building regulations.

Fascias and soffits

New uPVC fascia and soffit boards from Freefoam, with ventilated soffits to air the roof space.

Guttering

Tired or damaged guttering and downpipes replaced, in half-round, square or ogee and a range of colours.

About Mike Evans Windows

Mike Evans Windows was set up in 1999 by Mike Evans, a time-served carpenter and joiner. We're a small local company working mainly within 10 miles of Wrexham, and we've fitted for thousands of customers in and around the area.

Mike doesn't use sales reps on commission. He quotes every job himself, with design ideas and free, no-obligation advice. Our products come from major UK manufacturers, and the tradesmen on our conservatory builds are fully qualified and supply certificates for their work.
