About Build Pave & Scape

Build Pave & Scape has over 20 years' experience in building and hard landscaping. We take most pride in granite sett driveways, natural stone patios, courtyards, brickwork and natural stone walls.

We're Marshalls approved installers for block paving and patios. We've completed jobs across North Wales, the North West, Cheshire, Shropshire, the Midlands, London, the South and North of England, Northumbria and the Republic of Ireland.
