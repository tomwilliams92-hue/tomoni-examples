How we can help

Where surgery is needed, keyhole surgery is used wherever possible, so you get home and back to normal sooner.

Abnormal smears and colposcopy

A close look at the cervix in clinic. If abnormal cells need treating, this can usually be done at the same visit under local anaesthetic.

General gynaecology

Heavy or irregular periods, PMS, fibroids, polyps, ovarian cysts, prolapse, incontinence and problems of the menopause.

Pelvic pain and endometriosis

Investigation and treatment, from pain relief and medication to keyhole surgery to remove endometriosis.

Fertility and miscarriage

Investigations and first treatment for couples having difficulty conceiving, early pregnancy scans, and care after miscarriage.

Vulval skin conditions

Itching, soreness or skin changes properly diagnosed, so the right treatment can often start straight away.

Gynaecological cancer care

Private, consultant-led care for suspected and confirmed cancers, usually seen within 24 to 48 hours.

About Mr Peevor

Richard Peevor qualified with honours from the University of London in 2000, with a distinction in obstetrics and gynaecology. After research into HPV and cervical cancer, he trained as a gynaecological oncologist, completing his training in Cardiff.

He moved to North Wales to develop cancer services here, and is the first sub-specialty trained gynaecological cancer surgeon appointed in North Wales. With colleagues in Cardiff he has built the largest group of keyhole hysterectomy cases in Wales.

His private practice gives the same care he offers in the NHS, with one person looking after you from diagnosis to treatment and follow-up.
