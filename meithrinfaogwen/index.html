Childcare and fees

Prices from our current price list. Flying Start families save £12.50 a session in term time, and 3-year-olds get a further reduction.

Baby room

Morning or afternoon £30, full day £51.50, full week £235. Open-plan, with soft play and doors out to the play area.

Over-2s

Morning or afternoon £29, full day £50, full week £226. Play based on the Foundation Phase, with sand, water and messy play.

Meals

Cooked fresh on site every day. Breakfast £1.50, lunch £3, tea £1.50. Vegetarian and allergy options on request.

Cylch Meithrin

Mornings at Caban Ogwen, 9 to 11.30 am, £10.50. Meithrin Mwy on to 3 pm, £12.50.

Lunch time and after school clubs

At Caban Ogwen, Ysgol Llanllechid. Breakfast club £3.60, lunch time club £8, after school club 3 to 6 pm £11.

Holiday club

8 am to 6 pm in the school holidays. Full day £29.50, half day £18, with trips to the cinema, farms and the pier.

About Meithrinfa Ogwen

Meithrinfa Ogwen is a private day nursery in Bethesda, opened in April 2001 and run by Delyth Jones. We're a bilingual setting, registered for up to 41 children, and we've been members of Mudiad Meithrin since we opened.

Our building was purpose-built for children, with safety flooring, CCTV and a coded entry door. Meals are cooked fresh on site each day by our cook, and our staff are local and Welsh-speaking.
