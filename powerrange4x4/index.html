About Power Range

Mark Davies, a former Land Rover trained technician, set up Power Range in 1998. Too many North Wales drivers were travelling long distances to Land Rover main dealers, and he wanted to give them a real alternative.

Today the team has over 50 years' combined experience and is led by a factory trained Land Rover technician with over 35 years' experience. We're still 4x4 specialists, but we look after all makes of car and van, as well as trailers.
