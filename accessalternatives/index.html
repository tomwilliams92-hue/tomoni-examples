About Access Alternatives

Access Alternatives has over 40 years' experience in the mobility industry. We're fully accredited members of the Motability Scheme, both as a wheelchair accessible vehicle supplier and as an adaptation installer, and we sell to private buyers too.

Every used vehicle is individually selected and passes our inspection before it's advertised. We also supply and fit car adaptations, including hand controls, wheelchair and scooter boot hoists, person hoists, secondary driving controls and steering aids. Hand controls and secondary controls are fitted within 40 miles of Wrexham and Chester.
