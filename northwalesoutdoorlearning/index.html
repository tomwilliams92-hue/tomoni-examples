About North Wales Outdoor Learning

We train people to teach and lead outdoors: Forest School, Coastal School, curriculum-based outdoor learning and wellbeing in nature, plus other professional development.

Our experienced staff are members of the Outdoor Learning Training Network, which covers best practice, quality and standardisation across all registered trainers. We're an Awarding Body Centre with Agored Cymru and an ITC First Centre, so quality standards are kept and checked.

We can also help your setting build outdoor learning into everyday teaching, so it supports the Four Purposes across all six Areas of Learning and Experience, with ideas and resources for seasonal activities.
