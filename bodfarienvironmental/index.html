What we do

Products from our own woods and workshops, and contract work across North Wales and Cheshire.

Firewood and charcoal

Seasoned hardwood by the trailer load, nets of logs and kindling, barbecue and drawing charcoal, and charcoal chips for lighting stoves.

Tree nursery

Over 10,000 trees a year, mostly native hardwoods grown from local seed. Trees, guards and canes if you plant them yourself.

Hazel hurdles and greenwood

Hurdles to any size, ready-made or put up on site, plus stools, chairs, besoms, willow planters and bird tables.

Woodland management

Tree planting, thinning, felling and pruning, coppicing, rides and access tracks, waymarkers and information boards.

Play areas and willow

Play areas for schools, councils and private clients, in wood and natural materials, and living willow tunnels, domes and mazes.

Grounds, fencing and hedges

School and sports grounds, playgrounds and gardens, stock fencing, gates and stiles, and traditional mixed-species hedges.

About Bodfari Environmental

Bodfari Environmental is run by Alan and Helen Waterfield from The Warren, Bodfari, in the Clwydian Range Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty. We have 50 acres of mixed woodland, craft workshops and a small caravan and camp site.

We supply nets of logs, kindling and charcoal to B&Q through the BioRegional Charcoal Company, and our charcoal customers include Bangor University, Great Orme Mines and the Centre for Alternative Technology. We also run woodland, greenwood and coppice courses at the Woodland Skills Centre.
