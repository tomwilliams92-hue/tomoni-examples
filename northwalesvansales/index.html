About North Wales Van Sales

North Wales Van Sales was set up in 1995 and supplies new and used commercial vehicles across North Wales and the North West. Managing director John Rakestraw has worked as a commercial vehicle specialist since 1988, and is qualified as a Commercial Sales Consultant through Ford's training.

All used vehicles come with a parts and labour warranty, which can be extended for up to 2 years. If the van you want isn't in stock, our locator service will try to find it within 24 hours. Vans can be built to your needs, with tow bars, roof racks, sign writing and ply lining.
