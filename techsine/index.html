What we do

We prepare and carry out the full set of validation documents, from user requirements to performance qualification.

Validation master plans

We prepare validation master plans that set out how your equipment and systems will be qualified.

DQ, IQ, OQ and PQ

Design, installation, operational and performance qualification protocols, written and carried out.

User requirement specifications

User requirement specifications and procurement packages for all pharmaceutical equipment systems.

Temperature studies

Studies prepared, carried out and reported with GE Kaye Validator 2000 equipment, for autoclaves, freeze driers, fridges, freezers and incubators.

Process and packaging equipment

Vial and cartridge filling lines, packaging, filling and capping machines, blister packers, cartoners, tablet presses and coaters, and depyrogenation tunnels.

Clean rooms and utilities

Clean rooms and HVAC, water for injection, purified water, clean steam, compressed air, nitrogen and cold rooms.

About Techsine

Techsine is a full service validation consultancy based in North Wales. We've provided validation services to pharmaceutical companies across the UK and Europe.

Most of our work is preparing and carrying out validation master plans and qualification protocols, and running temperature studies on autoclaves, freeze driers, cold storage and incubators.
