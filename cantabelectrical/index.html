What we do

Electrical work of every size, from the smallest job to the largest, with free quotes and advice.

Homes

Rewires, extensions, power to garages and outbuildings, security lighting and Grade D smoke detectors.

Inspection and testing (EICR)

Periodic inspections and landlord safety checks. The report codes each item found and shows what it would cost to put right.

PAT testing, fire alarms and emergency lighting

Annual PAT testing with every appliance tagged. Fire alarm and emergency lighting design, fitting and testing, with a log book.

Shops and offices

Office installations and refurbishments, moves and new equipment, done with as little disruption as possible.

Industrial and farms

Three-phase installations, switchgear, cable tray, submains and lighting for plants, warehouses, factories and farms.

Churches and listed buildings

Lighting and power for churches, heritage sites, Cadw and listed buildings, and the five-yearly inspection they need.

About Cantab Electrical

Cantab Electrical is a family business based in Rhosymadoc, near Ruabon. We've provided electrical services since 2005, across Wrexham, Chester, North Wales, Cheshire, North Shropshire and the North West of England.

Our qualified electricians cover everything from survey and design to installation, testing, maintenance and certification. We're fully insured and Health and Safety accredited, and we've worked for Denbighshire County Council, Scottish Power and the National Trust.
