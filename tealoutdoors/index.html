Our programmes

Outdoor days and programmes for groups of any size, from one day to several.

Stag and hen days

Abseiling, rock climbing, wild swimming or hiking, put together into an action-packed day for your group.

Families and individuals

Outdoor days from a gentle introduction to a real adrenaline rush, planned around you.

Team building for businesses

Murmuration Training: one-day team building to multi-day outdoor programmes, for teams in the public and private sector.

Residentials for young people

Take Flight Adventure: 3 to 5-day trips with climbing, abseiling, gorge walking and hiking, in tents or dormitories.

Leadership days in school

Take Flight Leadership: a one-day outdoor programme in your school grounds for 11 to 18-year-olds.

Lift Off, for years 5 and 6

A team building and leadership workshop in school grounds, to get pupils ready for secondary school.

About TEAL

TEAL was founded in 2015 by Jodie Morris. We're a mobile team, bringing leadership training, team building and outdoor adventure to businesses, schools, groups and families.

Jodie's background is in personal development and outdoor education, with private companies and charities in the UK and overseas. Our courses also take in local environmental and conservation issues, and clients include Marks and Spencer, PwC, Rolls-Royce, Cadbury and the National Trust.
