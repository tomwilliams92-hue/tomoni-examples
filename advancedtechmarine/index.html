About AdvancedTech Marine

AdvancedTech Marine is the main local installer and supplier of marine electronics in North Wales and the North West, based in Menai Bridge on Anglesey.

With over 25 years in the marine trade locally, we take care and pride in all the electronics on board your boat. We work at all the marinas and boatyards in the area, including Pwllheli, Conwy, Deganwy and Holyhead, and offer a friendly, knowledgeable service in English or Welsh.
