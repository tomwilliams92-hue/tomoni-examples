What we do

Hire by the day or the week. Diggers and dumpers can be self-drive or come with an operator.

Grab lorry hire

Two six-wheel grab lorries. Each takes 12 cubic metres or 13.5 tonnes a load.

Diggers and mini diggers

A range of diggers, including mini diggers for the most awkward spaces.

Dumpers and small plant

Dumpers and mini dumpers, breaker packs, whacker plates, rollers and hydraulic peckers.

Groundwork and demolition

Site clearance, demolition, footings for extensions and general groundwork.

Driveways and landscaping

Driveways in tarmac or block paving, and landscaping.

Materials and tipping

We supply aggregates, recycled aggregates, stone, topsoil, road planings and tarmac. We take away soil, clay and rubble, but not household waste.

About Flintshire Grab Hire

We're a small family-run business based in Flintshire, North Wales, with over 30 years' experience of groundwork, demolition, site clearance, landscaping, extensions, footings, driveways, tarmac and block paving.

All our staff are qualified operatives with CSCS cards and the right operator licences. We're fully insured, with all the public liability insurance the work needs.
