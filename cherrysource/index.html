What we make

Parts in nitrile, natural rubber, EPDM, Neoprene, Viton, silicone and butyl. Food quality, WRAS and specialist grades are available too.

Rubber washers

Over 200 million a year. Lathe cut, punched, mandrel built, tube cut or moulded, from M3 to M36, plus BS746 meter and BSP fitting washers. Stock range online at rubberwashers.co.uk.

Rubber bands

The most extensive range on the market: natural, latex free, EPDM, pallet, X, printed and coloured bands, made to order. Stock bands online at rubberbandsuk.com.

Rubber mouldings

Low volume mouldings in a range of materials, hardnesses and colours, with reasonable tool costs and a quick turnaround.

O-rings

Made in the sizes and materials your application needs, in large or small quantities.

About CherrySource

CherrySource was formed in 2004 and is still run by the original partners, who have many years' experience in the rubber industry.

We make rubber washers and rubber bands, and made-to-order mouldings and O-rings in specialist sizes and materials. From our factory in North Wales we supply customers around the world, and we're known as a consistent, reliable supplier to industry.
