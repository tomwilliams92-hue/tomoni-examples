About a-pedwar

a-pedwar is a translation company based in a Welsh-speaking community in Gwynedd. Our clients have included the National Assembly for Wales, the Care Council for Wales, the Welsh Books Council, the Food Standards Agency, the Big Lottery Fund and county councils from Carmarthenshire to Flintshire.

One of our senior translators deals with each client personally. We work to tight deadlines without cutting corners, use the latest recognised terms, including TermCymru, and keep to any vocabulary you ask for.
