Our products

Instruments for hospitals, laboratories, offices, schools, homes, museums and industrial sites. If you can't find what you need, our technical team can design it with you.

Formaldehyde meters

The Formaldemeter htV-M, htV and 400ST measure formaldehyde in ppm and mg/m3 at the press of a button. The htV-M also logs for up to a month.

Indoor air quality monitors

Wireless monitors, networked around a building, that track temperature, humidity and other air quality readings over time.

Touch screen IAQ monitor

A stand-alone unit that shows every sensor in real time, with schedules, reports, alarms and notifications.

Base station and screens

Shows live readings from up to 10 monitors on any HDMI screen, or on our LCD screen.

Accessories

Desk stands, wall clamps, calibration standards, repeater units, dataloggers and the sensor calibrator.

Calibration and repairs

Factory calibration and servicing in Caernarfon, upgrades for older models, and design work for one-off projects.

About PPM Technology

PPM Technology designs and builds portable and fixed gas detection instruments at our own development and production facility on the Cibyn Industrial Estate in Caernarfon.

The PPM name goes back to 1993, when Dr Tom Parry Jones OBE set up PPM Ltd. In 1999 three employees bought the company and renamed it PPM Technology Ltd.

Our team works across production, R&D and administration. With a network of experienced distributors, our instruments are exported to over thirty countries on five continents.
