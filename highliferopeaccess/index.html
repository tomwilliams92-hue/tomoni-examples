For your home

Older Welsh houses were built with lime, and often suffer when they're repaired with cement. We use the skills we've learned on historic monuments to put them right. Everything on our maintenance and repair list is also available for homes.

Lime pointing

Lime mortars designed for your house, whether you're dealing with damp or just want a better option than cement.

Chimneys

Reflaunching, new pots, lead drip details, and repointing or rendering in lime mortar.

Roofing

Broken or slipped slates, lead flashings, Velux windows swapped, or a full reslate.

Core drilling

Clean holes through thick stone walls for plumbing, bathroom vents and more, across North Wales.

Lime washing and plastering

Lime wash and shelter coats instead of plastic paints, and lime plaster mixed to keep damp out.

Fireplaces

Original fireplaces uncovered, a hearth fitted and small areas of lime plaster. Ideal before a log burner goes in.

About Highlife

Highlife Rope Access is one of very few access companies working only in conservation. Our craftspeople and surveyors look after churches, cathedrals, castles, town halls and private estates, for clients including Cadw, Churches in Wales and architects across the country.

We're based in Bethesda, Gwynedd. Many homes here have been patched with cement over the years, which can lead to damp, so we now bring the same skills and materials to houses and cottages in the area.
