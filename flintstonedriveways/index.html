What we do

Driveways, paths and patios are all we do. Whether you're replacing a tired tarmac drive or redoing a patio, we lay hard-wearing, low-maintenance surfaces.

Pattern imprinted concrete

One continuous concrete surface with a stone, cobble, slate or brick look, in a wide range of colours. No loose blocks or weeds between joints.

Resin bound driveways

Natural stone set in clear resin. Smooth, with no loose stones, and water drains straight through. Blends from Autumn Gold to Silver Grey.

Cleaning and resealing

Deep cleaning, moss, algae and oil stain removal, small repairs and a fresh seal to bring back the colour of imprinted concrete.

About Flintstone

Flintstone Driveways design and install pattern imprinted concrete and resin bound driveways, paths and patios. We're based at Pistyll Farm, Nercwys, near Mold, and cover North Wales, including Flintshire, Denbighshire and Gwynedd, as well as Chester, Wrexham, Cheshire and the Wirral.

We're not general builders who also do drives. We've completed hundreds across the region, much of our work comes through word of mouth, and we also train new installers in the trade.
