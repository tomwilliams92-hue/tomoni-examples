Out with TEAL. School groups, teams and adventurers.

A mobile team with the outdoors at its heart.

TEAL was founded in 2015 by Jodie Morris. It is a mobile personal development organisation that brings leadership training and team building into the outdoors, and Jodie has over 11 years in personal development and outdoor education with private and charitable organisations in the UK and overseas.
