Inside the nursery. Reading corners, play tables and the rooms at Caban Ogwen.

A purpose-built nursery since 2001.

Meithrinfa Ogwen Cyf is a private day nursery opened in April 2001 and run by Mrs Delyth Jones. The building is purpose-built, with non-slip and safety flooring in the play area, CCTV and a coded entry door. We are a bilingual setting and members of Mudiad Meithrin.
