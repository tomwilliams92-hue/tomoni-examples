What we do

A friendly, efficient service, however simple or difficult the job.

Tree felling

Safe felling of trees, large and small, by our qualified team.

Crown reduction and thinning

Crown shaping, thinning and reduction for trees that have grown too big or too dense.

Pollarding and high hedges

Pollarding, and high hedges brought back down to a manageable height.

Emergency callouts

Call us about storm-damaged or dangerous trees.

Site clearance and stump grinding

Overgrown ground cleared and stumps ground out, with branches chipped on site.

Protected trees

Trees with a TPO or in a Conservation Area need council consent. We'll deal with the planning office for you.

About All Things Trees

All Things Trees was founded in 2006 by Ian and Jane Atkinson. We work from bases in Marford and Overton, near Wrexham, and cover North Wales, Cheshire and Shropshire.

As well as caring for your trees, we think about wildlife habitats and the wider landscape. We're members of the Arboricultural Association, insured for £5 million public liability, and all our work is done to BS 3998:2010 by NPTC qualified staff.
