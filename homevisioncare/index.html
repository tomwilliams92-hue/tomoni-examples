Who can have a visit

You can have an NHS eye test at home if you qualify for a free NHS eye test and can't get to a high street optician without help. You qualify for a free NHS eye test if any of these apply.

Aged 60 or over

Everyone aged 60 or over can have a free NHS eye test.

Diabetes or glaucoma

If you've been diagnosed with diabetes or glaucoma, or an eye specialist says you're at risk of glaucoma.

Glaucoma in the family

If you're 40 or over and a parent, brother, sister, son or daughter has glaucoma.

Sight loss

If you're registered partially sighted or blind, or have sight in one eye only.

Benefits or low income

If you get Income Support, Pension Guarantee Credit or another qualifying benefit, or have an HC2 or HC3 certificate.

Other reasons in Wales

For example, an eye problem that needs urgent attention, being profoundly deaf, or a referral from another health professional.
